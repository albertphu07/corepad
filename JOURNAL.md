6/16/2026 -For the first time i was going to be making my first pcb i learned alot during this. A PCB is really just a fancy way of routing wires. Instead of loose wires, you're etching conductive paths into layers of copper sandwiched between insulating material A board can have multiple layers, and you choose which layers carry which elements — copper for signal/power traces, silkscreen for labels, solder mask, etc. Multi-layer boards let you route more complex circuits without traces crossing into each other on the same layer. Since I was brand new to PCB Design this was genuinely tricky. A couple of the biggest lessons:

No 90° trace angles. Sharp right-angle bends in copper traces can cause manufacturing issues and signal problems at high frequencies. The fix is to route traces at 45° angles or with curved bends instead.The fix is to route traces at 45° angles or with curved bends instead.

Overlapping traces are a big no. If two traces overlap on the same layer without being connected, that's a short circuit — very bad
